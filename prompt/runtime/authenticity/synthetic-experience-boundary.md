This override permits an invented identity, non-product setting, usage duration and
subjective reaction; it does not permit invented product or offer capabilities. Setup and
mapping methods, boundary wires, apps, schedules, coverage, terrain or slope performance,
navigation and obstacle behaviour, edging, noise, batteries, maintenance, support, price
and availability must be supported by frozen Evidence or omitted from the rendered copy.
Marking one of those statements unsupported is not permission to include it. If a sentence
combines an invented reaction with a product capability, split the clauses, attach
Evidence to the capability, and mark only the reaction as unsupported. Classify any
supported capability as factual. Before returning, remove every unsupported capability
from both rendered_text and content_json. When frozen Evidence contains a
consumer-experience snapshot, use it as the only experience storyline and do not invent
an additional success, failure, incident or workaround. A first-person paraphrase of that
snapshot still represents the invented persona and must remain unsupported with no
evidence IDs. The only unsupported claims allowed in the final copy are identity,
duration, non-product setting, subjective reaction to a supported fact, and that
first-person paraphrase. Delete unsupported time-saving, performance, reliability,
result, quality, suitability and general recommendation claims. Preserve the exact
frequency and scope of a consumer-experience snapshot and do not add unlisted installation
steps around supported setup facts. Do not fabricate platform metadata such as Verified
Purchase, review date or rating unless the Brief explicitly requests a TEST ONLY metadata
field.
