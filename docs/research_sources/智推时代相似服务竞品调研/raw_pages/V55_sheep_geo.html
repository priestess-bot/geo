Frequently Asked Questions

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on optimizing your brand's visibility in AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Unlike traditional SEO which targets Google rankings, GEO ensures AI models accurately recommend and mention your brand when users ask relevant questions.
SHEEP-GEO uses our proprietary SHEEP Framework to analyze 5 dimensions: Semantic Coverage (how AI understands your brand), Human Credibility (trust signals), Evidence Structuring (content organization), Ecosystem Integration (presence in AI systems), and Performance Monitoring (conversion tracking). We monitor 8+ AI models in real-time.
SHEEP-GEO monitors 8+ mainstream AI models including ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), Perplexity AI, Microsoft Copilot, Llama (Meta), and Mistral AI. For the Asia market, we also support Chinese AI models like Doubao, Kimi, and DeepSeek.
SHEEP-GEO provides 24/7 real-time monitoring across all supported AI platforms. You receive instant email alerts when abnormal fluctuations in brand mentions are detected, ensuring you're always informed about your AI visibility status.
The SHEEP Framework is our proprietary 5-dimensional analysis system: S (Semantic Coverage) - AI recognition of your content; H (Human Credibility) - authority and trust signals; E (Evidence Structuring) - content organization; E (Ecosystem Integration) - presence in AI recommendation systems; P (Performance Monitoring) - conversion from AI recommendations.
