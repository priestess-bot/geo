Learn how Peec AI's Actions feature turns visibility data into clear, prioritized guidance

Actions analyzes hundreds of sources across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini, groups them into content-type clusters (like editorial listicles, Reddit discussions, or product pages), calculates competitive gaps, and gives you step-by-step guidance on what to create, optimize, or influence next.
Every Action gets a Relative Opportunity Score (1-3) based on two factors: how often AI models cite that source type, and how much competitors appear there versus you. Higher scores mean models use these sources frequently, but competitors dominate - making it a strong opportunity to improve your visibility.
On-Page Actions show gaps in content you control - like product pages, articles, or guides on your own site. Off-Page Actions cover editorial coverage, community discussions (Reddit, forums), and reference sources where you need to engage or get featured rather than publish directly.

Actions updates whenever your underlying visibility data refreshes - aligned with model refresh intervals. You'll see changes as new prompts are tracked or as model behavior shifts.
