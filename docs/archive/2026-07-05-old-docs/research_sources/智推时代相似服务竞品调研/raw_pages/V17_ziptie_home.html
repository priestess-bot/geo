ZipTie helps you understand your AI search performance across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity by tracking:

  • Brand mentions – Whether your brand appears in AI-generated answers
  • Sentiment analysis – How positively or negatively your brand is portrayed
  • Citation tracking – If your domain influences AI search engines
  • Competitive benchmarking – How your visibility compares to competitors
  • AI Overview coverage – Whether AI results are generated for your target queries
  • Full response text – Complete AI-generated answers for detailed analysis
  • Source attribution – Comprehensive lists of all domains cited by AI engines

You can analyze your performance at three levels:

  • Project overview – See overall trends across your entire project
  • Category level – Drill down using our automated tagging system
  • Query level – Examine individual prompts and their AI responses

Don't want to choose manually? Use our AI assistant to automatically select the right queries for you.

Prefer to build your own list? Here's what we recommend monitoring:

  • Category positioning queries – Track whether your brand appears in general searches for your product category. For example, if you're Tally (a form builder), monitor phrases like "best form generation software" to see if you're being recommended
  • Sentiment queries – Check if your brand receives positive mentions and recommendations for queries like: “is Tally recommended?”
  • Competitor comparison queries – Monitor how your brand stacks up against competitors in direct comparisons
  • Persona-specific variations – Add audience segments to these same query types. For instance: "best form generation software for beginners" or "Tally vs Google Forms for beginners."
