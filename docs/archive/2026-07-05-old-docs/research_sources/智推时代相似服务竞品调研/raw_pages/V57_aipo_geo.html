Frequently Asked Questions About GEO and AIPO

What brands ask us before getting started

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your brand's content, site structure, and digital footprint so that generative AI engines — including ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Chinese engines like DeepSeek, Doubao, Qwen, and Yuanbao — can find, understand, and cite your brand in their answers. Where traditional SEO competes for ranking in a list of links, GEO competes to become the answer itself.

What's the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links — the user still has to click through. GEO optimizes for being cited inside the AI-generated answer itself, where the user often never clicks through. Both depend on strong content fundamentals, but GEO puts far more weight on structured data, semantic clarity, third-party authority signals, and a consistent narrative across platforms. SEO and GEO are complementary, not competing.

What is AIPO, and how is it different from GEO?

AIPO (AI Platform Optimization) is YouFind's end-to-end framework built on top of GEO. GEO focuses on a single question — getting cited by AI. AIPO covers the full closed loop a brand actually needs: AI Visibility Audit, GEO Optimization, Content Strategy & Distribution, Performance Monitoring, and Strategic Analytics. GEO is one stage inside AIPO. AIPO is what turns AI visibility from a one-time project into a repeatable system.

Which AI engines does AIPO cover?

We monitor and optimize across the leading generative engines worldwide: ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Grok — alongside the major Chinese engines including DeepSeek, Doubao (ByteDance), Qwen (Alibaba), and Yuanbao (Tencent). This mix matters for global brands, and especially for businesses serving Chinese-speaking audiences abroad. Each engine cites sources differently, so we report your visibility engine by engine.

Is the audit really free? How long does it take?

Yes. Your initial AI Visibility Audit is free and takes about one minute. It shows where your brand currently stands across the major AI engines, where the citation gaps are, and how you compare to competitors. Deeper optimization, content production, and ongoing monitoring are paid services that build on the audit findings.

What if my brand isn't being mentioned by AI yet?

That's where most brands are today — and it doesn't mean your brand lacks credibility. It means AI engines haven't yet been given the structured, authoritative, and consistent signals they need to confidently cite you. The AIPO audit identifies the specific gaps — missing entity signals, weak third-party authority, content not structured for citation, narrative inconsistency across platforms — and the optimization stage closes them, one by one.
