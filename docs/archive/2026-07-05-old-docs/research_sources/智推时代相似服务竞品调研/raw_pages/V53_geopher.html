Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, is the process of improving how a brand, product, or website is mentioned, recommended, or cited by AI-driven search engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Unlike SEO, which focuses on keyword rankings, GEO focuses on AI visibility — how generative models understand and talk about your brand.

AI Visibility measures how often and how positively your brand appears within AI-generated responses. At Geopher.ai, we quantify this with our AI Visibility Index, which reflects your brand's presence across multiple AI engines including ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

We use our proprietary AI Visibility Index, a composite metric based on:

  • Frequency of brand mentions in AI-generated results

  • Contextual sentiment and accuracy of those mentions

  • Share of visibility vs. competitors

  • Citations and links detected across AI search outputs

  • The score helps brands understand how they perform across ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI engines.

You can increase AI visibility by:

  • Publishing trustworthy and well-structured content that AI models can easily reference.

  • Maintaining consistent brand mentions across verified domains, Wikipedia, and news sources.

  • Optimizing Knowledge Graph and metadata to make your brand machine-readable.

  • Using Geopher.ai insights to identify gaps and opportunities in how AI describes your brand.
